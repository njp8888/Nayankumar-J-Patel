SHEET 01 — ABOUT

Career Summary

Nayankumar J. Patel is an Assistant Professor of Mechanical Engineering at Parul Institute of Engineering and Technology — Diploma Studies, Vadodara, with a career spanning design engineering, production engineering, and academia since 2012. He holds an M.E. in CAD/CAM (Distinction) and a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering (Distinction) from Gujarat Technological University, and is currently pursuing a Ph.D. at Parul University.

His practical grounding in industry — as a Design Engineer and Production Engineer prior to teaching — informs a hands-on, application-first approach to mechanical design education, particularly in computer-aided design and manufacturing.
